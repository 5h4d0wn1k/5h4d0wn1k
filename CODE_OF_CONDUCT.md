# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This repository is a profile page and a small set of repository rules. People
interact with it through issues, pull requests, and discussions. The short
version of the standard: **be the kind of person you would want reviewing your
work.**

Expected behaviour:

- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Critique the change, not the person. *"This breaks X because Y"* is welcome;
  *"you are an idiot"* is not.
- Keep it useful. One concern per PR, real reproduction steps, no drive-by noise.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish other people's personal information, private
  emails, or credentials — including inside screenshots and logs.
- Accept "no" gracefully. A maintainer declining a change is not a personal
  attack.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, deliberate derailing, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the repository, and automated bulk PRs.
- Any conduct that would be unacceptable in a professional workplace.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages, reviews, and discussions
in this repository, and to any space where someone is representing this project.

## Reporting

Report a problem to **nikhilnagpure203@gmail.com**. Reports are read by the
maintainer only and handled privately. Include what happened, where, and links or
screenshots if you have them. Good-faith reports will never be held against you.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and history:

1. **A private note** — what was wrong and what is expected instead.
2. **A warning** — with a stated consequence for repeating it.
3. **A temporary block** — from issues and pull requests.
4. **A permanent block** — for severe or repeated violations.

Immediate permanent removal applies to harassment, doxxing, and threats. Comments
that violate this policy may be edited or deleted, and I will say when that
happens.
